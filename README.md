# ChefOS

This repository is the planning workspace for our restaurant operations product. Our goal is to build an optimized product for the users and workflows we choose—not to reproduce another vendor's product. ChefOS (chefos.us) is a market reference used to understand the category; it does not define our scope, roadmap, or architecture.

## Product direction

Start with a validated operator problem and deliver one reliable end-to-end workflow. Expand only when customer evidence and measurable outcomes support it. Product scope, initial customer, and first workflow are still open decisions; see [Product Direction](docs/product-overview.md).

## Repository contents

- [Product Direction](docs/product-overview.md) — our principles, open discovery questions, and competitor research boundary.
- [Product Requirements and Engineering Specification](docs/spec.md) — scope contract and cross-cutting quality requirements for features we choose.
- [Engineering Design and Architecture Decisions](docs/tech-stack.md) — architecture invariants, operating expectations, and ADR process.
- [Discovery and Delivery Plan](docs/build_plan.md) — evidence-based stages, readiness, and completion criteria.

## Project status

This repository currently contains design documents only. It has no application source, dependency manifest, production stack declaration, or runnable development setup. Technology and detailed feature scope remain intentionally open pending product and technical discovery.

## Reference research

Public product research for [ChefOS](https://chefos.us/) is summarized in [Product Direction](docs/product-overview.md). It is competitive context only. Its advertised features, connectors, and claims are not our requirements and should not be copied into a roadmap without independent user evidence.
